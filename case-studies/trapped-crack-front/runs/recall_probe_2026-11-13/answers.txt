Let \(x=a(z)\) be the crack front, with \(a(z)=a_0+\delta(z)\); the constant \(a_0\) drops out. Define the principal-value Hilbert transform
\[
\Lambda(z)=\frac{1}{\pi}\,\mathrm{PV}\!\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\frac{a'(s)}{s-z}\,ds
=\frac{1}{\pi}\,\mathrm{PV}\!\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\frac{a(s)-a(z)}{(s-z)^2}\,ds,
\]
which vanishes for a straight front.

My recollection of the Gao–Rice (1986) first-order result is
\[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
K_1(z)&=K_1^0\left[1+\frac{1-2\nu}{2(1-\nu)}\Lambda(z)\right],\\[2mm]
K_2(z)&=K_2^0\left[1+\frac{3-2\nu}{2(1-\nu)}\Lambda(z)\right]
+K_3^0\,\frac{3(1-2\nu)}{2(1-\nu)}\Lambda(z),\\[2mm]
K_3(z)&=K_3^0\left[1+\frac{1}{2(1-\nu)}\Lambda(z)\right]
+K_2^0\,\frac{3}{2(1-\nu)}\Lambda(z).
\end{aligned}}
\]
Equivalently,
\[
\mathbf K(z)=\bigl[\mathbf I+\Lambda(z)\mathbf C(\nu)\bigr]\mathbf K^0,
\]
with
\[
\mathbf C(\nu)=
\begin{pmatrix}
\dfrac{1-2\nu}{2(1-\nu)} & 0 & 0\\[2mm]
0 & \dfrac{3-2\nu}{2(1-\nu)} & \dfrac{3(1-2\nu)}{2(1-\nu)}\\[2mm]
0 & \dfrac{3}{2(1-\nu)} & \dfrac{1}{2(1-\nu)}
\end{pmatrix}.
\]
