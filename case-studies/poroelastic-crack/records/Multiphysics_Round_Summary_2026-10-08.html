Problem B: the drained crack tip

A crack in a fluid-saturated solid is loaded suddenly. In the bulk the response is undrained. At the tip the undrained pore pressure is singular, so the local drainage time r²/c goes to zero: at every t > 0 a zone of size √(ct) has drained. K is defined at r → 0, so K(0⁺) = Φ·K_outer, not K_outer.

Why the others missed it (from the run files): a formulation with no place for the tip (undrained field plus a face layer); a standard theorem that made K = 1 look exact; checks that verified the elastic solution but could not fail on the assumption; early stops (K3 11–22 min, DeepSeek 6–16 min of 50). GLM spent 90k–150k reasoning tokens on the same chain.

What GPT did: wrote the exact transform formulation, in which the drained and undrained limits are the two ends of one function; started from the definition of K; sent adversarial checks to subagents; refined before concluding.

The dimensional analysis behind it: the global group ct/L² is not enough; near the tip the field needs r/√(ct), and the two orders of limits are its two ends. The inner problem is self-similar, so K_tip/K_outer = Φ(η) does not depend on time.
