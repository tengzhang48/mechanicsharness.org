Multiphysics Crack Round

Four coupled-field crack problems (fluid-driven, poroelastic, piezoelectric, diffusive) were given closed-book to four AI agents, three runs each (GPT on B only; DeepSeek twice, first with reasoning off by mistake): 51 runs, 47 with an answer. Each answer was rerun, checked against a derived key, and graded blind against a profile, not a single score. All numbers below come from summary_data.json (the run logs, automatic checks and blind grades).

At a glance
Standard methods: mostly rightOn A, D and E, K3 and GLM usually reached the key (K3: all D parts right in 3 of 3 runs and all A key checks passed in 3 of 3; GLM all A key checks in 2 of 3). DeepSeek, rerun with reasoning on, joined them on A and D.
Harness: three corrections47 of 51 runs answered. DeepSeek ran with reasoning off (Cline without --thinking), so its results understate it. GLM's three missing or late runs hit the provider's 5-hour usage limit. The other two failures were Cline stops (compaction; a text-only turn).
